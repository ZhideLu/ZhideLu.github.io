Research Interests

My research focuses on quantum error correction (QEC), driven by the ultimate goal of realizing practical, large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computation. Specific research directions include:

Low-overhead quantum error correction

  • Designing low-overhead, high-threshold, hardware-efficient QEC codes
  • Developing low-latency, FPGA-based real-time decoders

Fault-tolerant logical operations and quantum compilation

  • Designing efficient fault-tolerant logical gates for Cornucopia codes
  • Compiling quantum algorithms into resource-efficient, fault-tolerant circuits tailored for high-rate QEC codes

Experimental quantum error correction

  • Co-designing quantum chips with experimental superconducting hardware groups to implement high-rate QEC codes, including optimizing layouts and coupler crossings
  • Co-designing atom-movement schedules with experimental neutral-atom groups to realize low-overhead error correction
